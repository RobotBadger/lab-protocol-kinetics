## Slide 13

Branching chain reactions

In some chain reactions, propagation involves a branching step.
This means that two chain carriers are produced from one
Now, the more chain carriers there are, the faster they are produced.
Branching propagation steps provide positive feedback

## Slide 26

Facts about nuclear fission

The reaction only works with a radioactive isotope of Uranium, U-235.
Naturally-occurring Uranium is a mixture of U-235 and U-238, typically <1% U-235.
The reaction requires a higher concentration of U-235 (“enriched”; around 10% for reactors in Europe; >90% for “weapons-grade” (for bombs).
The reaction requires a “critical mass” of material.
Scientists keep nuclear reactors under control

## Slide 38

c) Absorption

Is  (1.5 kfission[235U]) < or >  (kescape + kabsorb ) ? (ctd)

A nuclear reactor is run at around
1.5 kfission[235U]) - (kescape + kabsorb )≈0
It is kept at this value by controlling kabsorb using “control rods” (cadmium or boron) that absorb neutrons, and which may be lowered or raised in or out of the Uranium.
Scientists have built their own automated “negative feedback” system which raises the rods (decreasing kabsorb ) if the reaction begins to die away, and vice versa.
